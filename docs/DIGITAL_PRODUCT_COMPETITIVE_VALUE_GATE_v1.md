# DIGITAL PRODUCT COMPETITIVE VALUE GATE v1

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-09-09
Scope: every premium digital product before BUILD and again before RELEASE.

## Purpose

Prevent two opposite errors:

1. building an original product nobody wants;
2. entering a proven market with a product that is merely another copy of the category.

The operating doctrine is:

`PROVEN DEMAND -> PRE-BUILD COMPETITIVE BENCHMARK -> ORIGINAL BUILD -> POST-BUILD TRANSVERSAL AUDIT -> RELEASE`

A product may imitate a proven category and solve the same customer problem, but its files, formulas, copy, design, data and implementation must be original. Novelty is optional. Meaningful buyer value is mandatory.

## Gate A — PRE-BUILD COMPETITIVE BENCHMARK

Required before PRODUCT SPEC.

### Cohort
Use a minimum of 8 current comparable products when enough are available. The cohort must include:
- feature leaders;
- price leaders / low-cost substitutes;
- premium-priced competitors;
- at least one product with observable buyer validation when available;
- adjacent substitutes that solve the same job in another format.

Feature-rich listings without sales are useful for feature discovery, but must not be treated as proof that those features drive demand.

### Required outputs
1. Category demand evidence.
2. Current price band and premium ceiling.
3. Table-stakes matrix.
4. Competitor value-add matrix.
5. Buyer-friction hypotheses.
6. White-space / improvement hypotheses.
7. Frozen Product Value Thesis.

### Pre-build scoring
- Proven demand: 25
- Capturable price/value headroom: 15
- Table-stakes clarity: 10
- Meaningful improvement opportunity: 20
- Build feasibility: 10
- Portfolio leverage: 10
- Channel fit: 10

`>=80 BUILD PRIORITY`
`70-79 BUILD / TEST`
`60-69 WATCH`
`<60 REJECT`

Hard gate: there must be at least one credible buyer reason beyond cosmetic redesign.

## Gate B — POST-BUILD COMPETITIVE VALUE AUDIT

Required after BUILD and technical QA, before packaging/release.

The product is compared again against the same cohort plus any important new competitors discovered during build.

### B1. Table-stakes coverage — 20 points
The product must cover >=85% of critical table stakes for its chosen positioning. Missing a non-target feature does not count as failure if it was explicitly excluded from scope.

### B2. Outcome advantage — 20 points
At least one buyer outcome must be materially improved: better decision, less error, less time, more confidence, less leakage, better workflow, or better handoff.

### B3. Distinctive system advantage — 15 points
At least two non-cosmetic advantages must work together as a coherent workflow. A larger tab count alone does not count.

### B4. Usability / friction — 15 points
Onboarding, input clarity, error prevention, navigation, readable outputs and compatibility must be professional.

### B5. Technical correctness — 15 points
Critical formula and logic tests must pass 100%. No known critical error, broken reference or misleading calculation may ship.

### B6. Trust / claims — 5 points
No false uniqueness, unsupported compatibility, invented testimonials, fake scarcity or misleading financial/tax claims.

### B7. Visual / brand quality — 5 points
Coherent design, buyer-facing language, hierarchy, packaging and screenshots.

### B8. Value / price — 5 points
Price must be defensible relative to the cohort and buyer outcome.

Post-build result:
- `>=90 PREMIUM RELEASE PASS`
- `85-89 RELEASE PASS` only if no red finding remains
- `75-84 REWORK`
- `<75 BLOCK RELEASE`

## Gate C — TRANSVERSAL AUDIT

A product cannot be declared FINAL from a single-dimensional QA pass.

Audit independently across:
1. Market fit.
2. Competitive parity.
3. Differentiation.
4. Financial/math correctness.
5. Edge cases / invalid inputs.
6. Workflow logic.
7. UX and onboarding.
8. Visual hierarchy and brand consistency.
9. Compatibility and portability.
10. Buyer-facing deliverables.
11. Claims, legal/trust wording and license.
12. Channel packaging.
13. Support burden / failure modes.
14. Performance measurement readiness.

Findings use severity:
- RED: blocks release.
- AMBER: must be fixed or explicitly accepted with rationale before release.
- GREEN: pass.

A final product requires ZERO RED findings.

## Gate D — BRAND PLUS

Every premium family must preserve a recognizable value signature.

For the Freelancer family the initial signature is:

`PRICE -> PROTECT -> NEGOTIATE -> LEARN`

Meaning:
- PRICE: calculate a sustainable rate and quote;
- PROTECT: expose floors, fees, hidden hours and margin risk;
- NEGOTIATE: budget-fit, safe-discount and change-request guardrails;
- LEARN: compare quote vs actual and feed the result into Pricing Memory.

The signature is a workflow, not a decorative slogan. If a release does not execute the relevant stages, it may not claim the family advantage.

## Governance

- Pre-build and post-build scores are frozen with dates and evidence URLs.
- Do not rewrite a historical benchmark to make a later result look better.
- New competitor discoveries may trigger a new audit version, not silent score changes.
- Buyer transaction data outranks listing feature claims.
- Product performance after release feeds back into future benchmark weights.
