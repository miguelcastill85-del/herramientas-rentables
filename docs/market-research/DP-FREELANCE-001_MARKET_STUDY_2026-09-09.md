## Research objective

Select the first weekly premium product using proven marketplace demand, not novelty. Compare several adjacent digital-product archetypes and choose the candidate with the best combination of buyer evidence, capturability, price/value headroom, production speed and portfolio leverage.

## Current market evidence

### Archetype A — Freelancer pricing / rate / quote calculator

Observed Etsy market pages:
- `Freelance Pricing`: 1,000+ items.
- Standalone examples span roughly US$7 to US$28 in current search results.
- `Project Quote Calculator for Freelancers` appears around US$16 with 500+ visible review-proxy count in the market result.
- `Cost Pricing Calculator for Beauty Business` appears around US$8–16 with 2,000+ visible review-proxy count.
- Current products already offer rate calculation, tax/overhead assumptions, billable capacity, project pricing, dashboards, scenario analysis, project profitability and effective hourly rate.

Interpretation: demand is proven; generic features alone are not differentiation. A strong product can still enter by combining a better workflow, professional packaging and pricing feedback loop.

### Archetype B — Freelancer business bundle

Observed Etsy results:
- `Freelancer Business Bundle ... Client Management Invoice Bookkeeping Pricing ...` appears around US$22 with roughly 3,000+ visible review-proxy count.
- Related small-business/freelancer bundles appear around US$13–23 and combine bookkeeping, CRM, pricing, invoice and dashboards.

Interpretation: strong proven premium demand and excellent bundle economics, but higher build and QA complexity makes it a better week-4 family culmination than the first standalone release.

### Archetype C — Small business operations bundle

Observed Etsy results:
- Broad `Small Business Bundle` and `Small Business Spreadsheet Templates` pages show 5,000+ items.
- A leading small-business bundle appears around US$15 and shows roughly 10,000+ review-proxy count in market search results.
- One detailed listing includes bookkeeping, business/order tracker, pricing calculator, bill calendar, Excel + Google Sheets, instructions and tutorial; the item itself shows hundreds of reviews and thousands of favorites.

Interpretation: very large validated market, but broader scope, more support burden and weaker immediate fit with the existing freelancer funnel than Archetype A.

### Archetype D — Small business bookkeeping

Observed Etsy results:
- Broad bookkeeping pages show 5,000+ items.
- Multiple listings show thousands of visible review-proxy counts, including examples around US$1, US$5, US$10 and US$12.

Interpretation: extremely proven demand but severe price compression and high commoditization. Good future family/vertical product, not first choice.

### Archetype E — Client/CRM tracker

Observed Etsy results:
- `Clients Tracker` and CRM market pages show 5,000+ items.
- Leading visible entries show thousands of review-proxy counts; price spans roughly US$2 to US$12+ for spreadsheet products.

Interpretation: proven demand with substantial price pressure. Strong adjacent module for the freelancer family.

## Product decision

### Product ID
`DP-FREELANCE-001`

### Working commercial promise
**Know what to charge before you quote — and learn whether the project actually paid afterward.**

### Customer
Freelancers, consultants and independent service professionals who quote fixed-price, hourly or retainer work and want to avoid underpricing.

### Core job-to-be-done
Turn income goals, capacity, project scope and cost assumptions into a defensible price; then compare the quote with actual project performance so future pricing improves.

## Table stakes — required, not differentiators

The current market already contains many of these, so they do not count as unique advantage:
- hourly/day/project rate calculations;
- taxes/overhead as user-entered assumptions;
- billable capacity;
- dashboards;
- scenario analysis;
- Excel and/or Google Sheets compatibility;
- instructions and sample data;
- project profitability/effective hourly rate.

## Value-add package for our version

Our first release will emphasize workflow quality rather than claiming category invention:

1. **Sustainable Rate Builder** — work backward from take-home goal, overhead, non-billable time and user-entered reserves.
2. **Project Quote Builder** — hours, complexity, revision allowance, external costs, contingency, target margin and optional rush/discount adjustments.
3. **Profit Protection** — minimum sustainable price, recommended price and maximum discount before a selected margin/floor is violated.
4. **Quote vs Actual Learning** — quoted hours/price versus real hours/costs, actual effective rate and actual margin.
5. **Pricing Memory** — completed project rows feed a simple view of which project/client types consistently outperform or underperform assumptions.
6. **Decision Signals** — clear flags such as HEALTHY / REVIEW / UNDERPRICED rather than exposing only raw arithmetic.
7. **Professional Start Here + demo dataset** — buyer can understand the product without reading a long manual.
8. **Native release QA** — Excel package plus a separately validated Google Sheets path rather than merely assuming imported compatibility.
9. **Currency-agnostic core** — avoid country-specific tax claims; user controls reserve assumptions.
10. **Premium brand system** — coherent cover, screenshots, guide, file names, versioning and listing assets.

None of these elements should be marketed as unique unless future competitor research supports that claim. The differentiator is the integrated quality and outcome.

## Initial price hypothesis

Etsy initial price test: **US$14.90**.

Rationale:
- current standalone comparable listings range from low single digits to roughly US$28;
- project quote/rate tools cluster around the mid-teens in visible examples;
- US$14.90 avoids the US$1–5 commodity tier while remaining credible for a new shop without review history.

Etsy Chile current base fee structure checked 2026-09-09:
- listing: US$0.20;
- transaction: 6.5%;
- Etsy Payments processing for Chile: 6.5% + US$0.30;
- optional ads, taxes/VAT or other applicable charges are separate.

At US$14.90, the basic transaction + processing + listing charges are approximately US$2.44 before VAT/optional advertising, leaving roughly US$12.46 before those additional items. This is only an initial unit-economics estimate; actual payout telemetry will replace it after launch.
