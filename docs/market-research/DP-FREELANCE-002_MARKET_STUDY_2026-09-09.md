## Thesis

Do not enter the generic low-price CRM category with another contact list. Enter the proven client/project tracker market with a narrower economic outcome: show which clients and projects actually pay well after time, costs and collected revenue are considered.

## Current Etsy evidence

Observed market evidence on 2026-09-09:

- `Freelance Client Tracker`: 3,000+ items. Visible products include a freelancer tracker with 600+ review-proxy count and client/invoice tracker products with roughly 10,000+ review-proxy counts. Low-price competition is common around US$2–6.
- `Client Tracker Sheets`: 5,000+ items. A multi-project management tracker appears around US$18 with 500+ review-proxy count, while broad CRM trackers show thousands of review proxies.
- `Project Profit`: specific profitability products include a Client Profitability Tracker / Project Profit Spreadsheet around US$12.99 and project expense/profit products around US$8+ with 1,000+ review-proxy counts.
- `Invoice Tracker and Payment`: 1,000+ items, including higher-featured client/invoice trackers around US$11–12 and strong review-proxy evidence.

Interpretation: the generic client-tracker category is large but price-compressed. The stronger entry point is the intersection of client management + project economics, where a professional spreadsheet can justify a mid-teens price by solving a more valuable decision problem.

## Hard gates

- G1 ORIGINAL_AND_LEGITIMATE: PASS. We can create original tracking logic, formulas, workflow, documentation and design.
- G2 POSITIVE_UNIT_ECONOMICS: PASS. Comparable higher-featured spreadsheets support roughly US$8–18+ pricing; digital fulfillment remains low-cost.

## Proposed buyer job

**Know which clients and projects are worth more of your time — based on actual revenue, hours, costs and payment status.**

## Table stakes

Required but not differentiating:
- client database;
- project status;
- task/deadline fields;
- quoted amount;
- payment status;
- simple dashboard;
- Excel delivery;
- filtering and clean instructions.

## Value-add direction

1. Client-level profitability rollup from multiple completed projects.
2. Project effective hourly rate based on actual hours and collected revenue.
3. Quote-vs-actual hours error.
4. Payment collection status separated from quoted/revenue values.
5. Client profitability tier: STRONG / REVIEW / LOW RETURN.
6. Service-type performance summary.
7. Capacity view: active workload + committed hours.
8. Direct integration path with DP-FREELANCE-001 through compatible project fields, without making either standalone product depend on the other.
9. Bundle-ready architecture for DP-FREELANCE-004.

## Scope guardrail

Do not turn v1 into full CRM software. Exclude email automation, legal contracts, bookkeeping, tax filing, inventory and external APIs. The product wins through project economics and usable workflow, not maximum feature count.

## Initial price hypothesis

Test band: **US$12.90–16.90**. Freeze exact launch price after product-spec benchmarking against current higher-featured comparables.
