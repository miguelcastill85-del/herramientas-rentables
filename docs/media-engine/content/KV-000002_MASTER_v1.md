# KV-000002 MASTER v1 — Discount-volume math

Status: DRAFT_FOR_QA

Franchise: Errores caros

## Core lesson

A percentage discount and the extra sales required to preserve the same gross contribution are not equal percentages. The answer depends on the margin before discount.

## Canonical example

Assumptions:

- selling price: 100
- variable cost: 60
- contribution/gross amount per unit before discount: 40
- discount: 10%
- new selling price: 90
- variable cost unchanged: 60
- new contribution per unit: 30

To preserve the original total contribution of 40:

40 / 30 = 1.3333

Therefore the business needs about 33.3% more units under these assumptions.

Do NOT generalize this to every 10% discount. The result changes with margin/cost structure.

## QA notes

- Use "contribución" or "ganancia bruta por unidad bajo el supuesto simplificado"; avoid calling it net profit.
- Preserve the assumption that variable cost stays at 60.
- Recalculate 40/30 before publication.
- No earnings promise.
