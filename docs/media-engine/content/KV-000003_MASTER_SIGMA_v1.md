## Core lesson

Break-even turns fixed costs, price and variable cost into a minimum unit-sales target under explicit assumptions.

## Canonical formula

`Break-even units = Fixed costs / (Unit selling price - Unit variable cost)`

Canonical example:

- Fixed costs: 1,000
- Unit price: 50
- Variable cost per unit: 30
- Unit contribution: 20
- Break-even: 1,000 / 20 = 50 units

Approved takeaway: under these assumptions, 50 units cover the fixed costs represented in the example; this is not a guarantee of future profit or cash solvency.
