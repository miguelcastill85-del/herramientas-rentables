# MEDIA ENGINE WORKLOG v1.1 GROWTH PATCH

## Authority

This document is the operating authority for the Herramientas Rentables Media Engine until superseded by a versioned replacement.

GitHub is the durable source of truth. Chats may research, draft, analyze and execute work, but important decisions, schemas, states, learnings and artifacts must be materialized in the repository.

## Mission

Build a scalable media business that converts verified, useful knowledge into original social content, grows a recurring audience, moves part of that audience toward owned assets, and later monetizes repeated audience problems through digital products or services.

Initial distribution channels: Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.

Initial additional-cost target: USD 0.

## Core promise

Publish information that helps people understand how money, business, work and practical technology actually function so they can make better decisions.

Avoid empty motivation, generic curiosity, copied content and fake "secrets".

## Operating principle

Do not choose between rigor and speed. Run two synchronized tracks:

### FOUNDATION_ENGINE
Builds and hardens the durable system: brand, Knowledge Vault, source rules, templates, QA, analytics, repository structure, automation and backups.

### GROWTH_LAB
Publishes and tests as soon as the Minimum Viable Brand and the first 15-20 verified ideas are ready. It measures discovery, conversion, sharing and retention while FOUNDATION_ENGINE continues improving the infrastructure.

## Launch rule

The system does NOT wait for 100 ideas.

Launch eligibility requires:

1. Minimum Viable Brand frozen.
2. Editorial boundary frozen.
3. 15-20 verified seed ideas.
4. Three minimum viable formats: Reel, carousel and professional text post.
5. Content QA active.
6. Profiles prepared to convert profile visits into followers.
7. Measurement fields ready.

The Knowledge Vault then grows continuously toward 100, 500, 1,000 and beyond.

## Content states

Allowed states:

- DRAFT
- RESEARCHED
- VERIFIED
- APPROVED
- SCHEDULED
- PUBLISHED
- MEASURED
- LEARNED
- ARCHIVED
- REJECTED

No ambiguous intermediate language is authoritative.

## Content pipeline

DISCOVER -> RESEARCH -> VERIFY -> SCORE -> CREATE MASTER PIECE -> ADAPT PER PLATFORM -> QA -> SCHEDULE/PUBLISH -> CONFIRM -> MEASURE -> LEARN -> AMPLIFY OR ARCHIVE.

## Risk-tier QA

### QA-A: high risk
Financial, legal, health, regulated topics, sensitive numbers or strong causal claims. Requires strongest source and context review.

### QA-B: medium risk
Business, marketing, studies, statistics and technical claims. Requires reliable evidence and interpretation review.

### QA-C: low risk
Productivity, simple tool usage, general workflows and non-sensitive explanatory content. Requires accuracy, originality and brand review.

## Platform missions

### Instagram
Primary discovery engine. Optimize non-follower reach, shares, saves, profile visits and follows. Reels and carousels are primary formats. Use platform-native testing features when available and suitable.

### Facebook
Redistribution and community engine. Optimize shares, Reels, discussion and useful explanatory content.

### LinkedIn
Authority engine. Fewer, denser posts are acceptable. Optimize professional relevance, credibility, qualified profile visits, conversation and site traffic. Do not automate fake conversation or low-effort engagement.

## Content portfolio

Default planning mix:

- 60% evergreen
- 25% responsive/timely
- 15% deliberate experiments

The mix may change from evidence, not intuition alone.

## Explore / Exploit

At the beginning, exploration is deliberately high because there are no proven winners.

Initial working allocation:

- 60% EXPLORE
- 40% EXPLOIT

After repeated winners emerge, the system may move toward approximately 30% exploration / 70% exploitation, while never reaching zero exploration.

## Discovery Lab

Every experiment must register:

- hypothesis
- variable changed
- control/reference
- target metric
- platform
- sample size or minimum observations
- start/end
- result
- decision

Avoid changing hook, topic, format, CTA and timing simultaneously when causal learning matters.

## Winner Amplification Engine

A winning knowledge unit is not considered exhausted after one post.

A repeated winner may produce a content tree:

- alternate Reel
- carousel
- Facebook explanation
- LinkedIn analysis
- part 2
- case study
- checklist
- tool
- article
- newsletter
- product hypothesis

Amplification must preserve factual integrity and avoid repetitive spam.

## Fast Follow rule

Operational variables can change quickly when evidence appears:

- hook
- presentation
- length
- follow-up angle
- format
- sequencing

Strategic variables change more slowly:

- master brand
- core promise
- editorial boundary
- product architecture
- business model

This prevents both rigidity and strategic drift.

## Distribution Engine

Publication is incomplete without a plausible distribution mechanism.

For each important piece, consider:

- organic recommendation potential
- shareability
- repost potential
- collaboration
- expert participation
- relevant mentions
- useful conversation
- cross-platform adaptation
- owned-channel routing

No paid distribution is required for initial validation.

## Collaboration Engine

Use legitimate value exchange with relevant experts, microcreators, freelancers, entrepreneurs, tools and educational pages.

Do not buy engagement or manufacture fake endorsements.

## Profile Conversion Engine

Each profile must answer immediately:

1. What is this account about?
2. Why should I follow it?
3. What value will I receive repeatedly?
4. Where can I go for deeper/free utility?

Use pinned content to demonstrate value, explain the promise and orient new visitors.

## Series and content franchises

Favor recognizable recurring series over disconnected posts.

Candidate franchises may include:

- Lo que nadie te explica
- Errores caros
- Cómo funciona realmente
- El número que importa
- Herramienta que vale conocer
- Una idea para guardar

Names are not frozen until Brand Architecture v1 closes.

## Growth loops

The system must seek loops, not only a linear funnel.

Example social loop:

content -> share -> new viewer -> profile -> follow -> another useful piece -> share.

Example owned loop:

content -> free tool -> useful result -> share/result referral -> new user -> tool.

## Primary metrics

Do not use follower count alone.

Track where available:

- non-follower reach
- impressions/reach
- profile visits
- follow conversion
- shares/reach
- saves/reach
- comments/reach
- clicks/reach
- watch retention
- returning audience
- owned-audience conversion
- winner rate
- winner replication rate
- collaboration reach

## Decision cadence

### Fast operational review
Use recent evidence to exploit obvious winners or correct obvious failures without waiting for the monthly strategy review.

### Weekly review
Review publishing reliability, best/worst pieces, experiments, comments/questions, backlog and next week.

### Monthly review
Review positioning, growth, platform roles, content families, owned-audience conversion, costs and product signals.

Do not change the core strategy from one viral post.

## Cost governance

Classify every dependency:

- FREE
- PAID_OPTIONAL
- PAID_REQUIRED

Before adding a paid dependency, record cost, free limits, purpose, alternatives, lock-in and rollback path.

No unapproved paid service should become necessary for basic operation.
