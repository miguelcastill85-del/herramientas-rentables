# CONTENT_QA v1

Status: ACTIVE

## PASS requirements for every piece

### Claim integrity
- Every material claim maps to one verified KV item or is visibly labeled as opinion/hypothesis.
- Wording does not become stronger than the source.
- Caveats recorded in the KV item are preserved when material.

### Numerical integrity
- Recalculate all arithmetic independently.
- State the base of every percentage when ambiguity is possible.
- Distinguish revenue, gross profit/contribution, margin, markup, cash flow and net profit.

### Originality
- The piece must add our explanation, example, calculation, framing or synthesis.
- Do not copy another creator's post structure/content line by line.
- Third-party visuals require usage rights or replacement by original graphics.

### Brand fit
- Viewer should gain useful knowledge even without clicking or buying.
- Avoid fake secrets, conspiratorial framing and get-rich-quick language.
- Selling is secondary during discovery cohorts.

### Platform fit
- Instagram: discovery/save/share optimized.
- Facebook: share/discussion optimized.
- LinkedIn: authority/professional relevance optimized.

### Conversion fit
- The profile promise and CTA must agree with the content.
- Do not send every post to a product.

## QA tiers

### QA-A
Financial/legal/health/regulated/high-consequence claims.
Requires primary or strongest available authority, current-date check when relevant, caveat review and independent numerical verification.

### QA-B
Business, behavioral studies, statistics, technology/security claims.
Requires reliable source, interpretation review and numerical verification where applicable.

### QA-C
Low-risk workflows, productivity, arithmetic examples and tool tips.
Requires correctness, originality and clear assumptions.

## Automatic rejection reasons

- UNSOURCED material claim presented as fact
- fabricated statistic
- fabricated quotation
- false causality
- percentage without defined base when material
- copied content
- unsupported "nobody wants you to know" framing
- misleading earnings promise
- contradiction with active KV authority

## Correction protocol

If an error escapes publication:

1. create INC ID;
2. preserve original record;
3. correct KV if needed;
4. correct/remove social piece depending severity and platform mechanics;
5. issue visible clarification when the error is material;
6. add a prevention check to QA.
