## 1. Brand role

SIGMA is the master media and knowledge brand of the Media Engine.

Its purpose is to make useful complexity understandable.

SIGMA should feel like a modern knowledge company, not a motivational account, generic news page, luxury-fashion brand, meme account or catalog of products.

## 2. Positioning statement

For Spanish-speaking people who want to make better decisions in money, business, work and technology, SIGMA is a practical knowledge media brand that identifies what matters, verifies it and explains it clearly enough to use.

## 3. Brand promise

> **SIGMA convierte información compleja o poco explicada en conocimiento práctico para tomar mejores decisiones.**

This is the positioning authority. It can be shortened for platform copy but not contradicted.

## 4. Primary tagline

> **Lo que importa, explicado.**

Use as the default compact brand signature.

## 5. Secondary signature

> **Entender mejor. Decidir mejor.**

Use when a more action-oriented line is appropriate.

Do not stack both taglines in small assets.

## 6. Core value proposition

Every important SIGMA piece should deliver at least one of these outcomes:

1. reveal a mechanism the audience commonly overlooks;
2. make a useful calculation understandable;
3. expose a costly decision error;
4. explain how a familiar product/system actually works;
5. show a practical tool or workflow;
6. convert evidence into an actionable implication;
7. reduce confusion before a decision.

A post that is merely entertaining but provides none of these outcomes should not represent the core brand.

## 7. Audience

Primary audience:

- freelancers;
- entrepreneurs and small-business owners;
- professionals;
- digitally active consumers;
- people learning to use AI and technology more intelligently;
- people interested in pricing, negotiation, money and decision quality.

Audience is defined by a behavior, not by age: people who prefer understanding before acting.

## 8. Brand personality

Working archetype mix:

- **70% Sage** — evidence, clarity, explanation, precision.
- **20% Explorer** — discovery, curiosity, what others overlook.
- **10% Challenger** — respectfully question common assumptions.

SIGMA is:

- clear;
- intelligent;
- calm;
- useful;
- curious;
- precise;
- contemporary;
- confident without arrogance;
- premium without pretension.

SIGMA is not:

- guru-like;
- mystical;
- macho;
- alarmist;
- elitist;
- excessively academic;
- salesy;
- sarcastic as a default;
- sensationalist.

## 9. Voice system

### Default voice

Write as an expert explainer speaking to an intelligent non-specialist.

Prefer:

- short opening;
- concrete claim;
- example or number;
- mechanism;
- implication;
- useful conclusion.

### Tone by context

**Educational:** direct, patient, rigorous.

**Counterintuitive:** surprising but evidence-led.

**Warning:** calm and specific, never fear-based.

**Commercial:** transparent and restrained.

**LinkedIn:** professional, analytical and human.

**Instagram:** immediate, visual and memorable.

**Facebook:** conversational, explanatory and shareable.

## 10. Language rules

### Preferred language

Use phrases such as:

- “Lo importante aquí es…”
- “La cuenta cambia cuando…”
- “Parece pequeño, pero…”
- “Esto ocurre porque…”
- “En la práctica…”
- “Antes de decidir, mira esto.”
- “El dato útil es…”
- “No es un secreto: está ahí, pero casi nadie lo calcula.”

### Avoid

Do not use without strong evidence:

- “nadie quiere que sepas esto”;
- “te están robando”;
- “hazte rico”;
- “dinero fácil”;
- “garantizado”;
- “100% seguro”;
- “el algoritmo te oculta”;
- “este truco cambia tu vida”;
- fabricated urgency;
- fake exclusivity.

Never present opinion as verified fact.

## 11. Hook doctrine

A SIGMA hook can be strong, but the body must fully justify it.

Good structures:

- consequence: “Un 10% de descuento puede costarte mucho más de 10% de beneficio.”
- contradiction: “Facturar más no siempre significa ganar más.”
- mechanism: “Hay una razón matemática por la que este precio parece barato.”
- overlooked variable: “Antes de aceptar este trabajo, falta un número.”
- practical question: “¿Cuánto tienes que vender para no perder dinero?”

Forbidden structure:

Create curiosity by asserting a conspiracy, certainty or outcome that the evidence does not support.

## 12. Editorial boundary

Core domains:

- pricing, margins and costs;
- freelancing and small business;
- sales and negotiation;
- consumer economics;
- marketing mechanisms;
- decision psychology;
- AI and practical technology;
- digital tools;
- productivity with measurable utility;
- fees, rules and mechanisms that affect real choices;
- practical data interpretation.

Exclude as core strategy:

- generic motivation;
- celebrity gossip;
- partisan bait;
- random trivia;
- copied/reposted content;
- pseudo-science;
- get-rich-quick content;
- “sigma male”/alpha-beta content;
- low-effort AI filler.

## 13. Editorial franchises

Initial franchise system:

1. **Lo que nadie te explica** — hidden or poorly explained mechanisms.
2. **Errores caros** — decisions that cost money, margin, time or opportunity.
3. **Cómo funciona realmente** — deconstruct familiar systems and offers.
4. **El número que importa** — one calculation or metric that changes the decision.
5. **Herramienta que vale conocer** — practical digital utility.
6. **Una idea para guardar** — compact high-save insight.
7. **Caso / experimento** — evidence, examples and deconstruction.

Franchise names are subordinate to SIGMA. They never become competing master brands without an explicit spin-out decision.

## 14. Visual system

### Principle

Premium means restraint, not decoration.

SIGMA should be recognizable through high contrast, disciplined spacing, geometry, strong typography and selective metallic/gold accents.

### Canonical digital palette

- **SIGMA Ink:** `#111214` — primary text/background.
- **SIGMA Ivory:** `#F7F5F0` — primary light background.
- **White:** `#FFFFFF` — utility/background.
- **SIGMA Gold:** `#B88A2A` — accent only.
- **SIGMA Deep Gold:** `#7A571A` — accessible dark-gold text/accent where needed.
- **Graphite:** `#42454A` — secondary text/structure.

Contrast validation against SIGMA Ivory:

- Ink ≈ 17.2:1 — excellent for text.
- Graphite ≈ 8.8:1 — excellent for text.
- Deep Gold ≈ 6.0:1 — suitable for normal text under WCAG AA.
- Gold ≈ 2.9:1 — **accent only**, not normal body text on light backgrounds.

### Color ratio

Default visual composition:

- 70–85% Ink/Ivory/White;
- 10–20% Graphite/neutral structure;
- 5–15% Gold accents.

Do not turn every asset into a metallic-gold luxury poster. Content readability is more important than ornament.

## 15. Typography

The canonical logo typography is part of the selected logo artwork and must not be approximated casually.

Supporting system fonts for content:

- **Headline:** Manrope, weight 600–800.
- **Body:** Inter, weight 400–600.

Both are chosen for clarity, modernity and broad digital usability.

Rules:

- sentence case is preferred for explanatory headlines;
- all caps only for small labels/categories;
- no decorative script fonts;
- never use more than two supporting type families in one asset;
- body copy must remain readable on mobile at first glance.

Therefore:

- do not claim a particular logo file is canonical;
- content templates may reserve a logo-safe area but should not bake in an unverified logo.

Once ingested, store original/master plus exported profile-safe variants and record hashes.

### General logo behavior after ingest

- preserve aspect ratio;
- no stretching;
- no recoloring outside approved variants;
- no glow/shadow added ad hoc;
- keep clear space around mark;
- maintain a monochrome fallback;
- avatar version must remain legible at small size.

## 17. Image and graphic language

Prefer:

- clean diagrams;
- numbers as focal points;
- simple geometric composition;
- one strong idea per frame;
- generous negative space;
- evidence/source labels when useful;
- charts designed for comprehension, not decoration.

Avoid:

- generic business stock photography as default;
- fake dashboards;
- excessive neon/crypto aesthetics;
- visual clutter;
- pseudo-luxury marble/gold everywhere;
- masculine “sigma grindset” imagery.

## 18. Social visual formats

Minimum viable formats:

### Reel

- hook visible immediately;
- large readable text;
- one concept;
- brand mark subtle;
- strong final implication/CTA.

### Carousel

- slide 1: hook;
- slide 2: problem/context;
- middle: mechanism/example;
- penultimate: implication;
- final: concise takeaway + optional follow/save CTA.

### Professional text post

- clear first sentence;
- short paragraphs;
- example or evidence;
- implication;
- no forced engagement bait.

## 19. CTA system

Priority order during audience-building:

1. understand the idea;
2. save/share if genuinely useful;
3. follow for recurring value;
4. visit a free owned utility;
5. paid product only when contextually relevant.

Approved CTA examples:

- “Guárdalo para cuando tengas que calcularlo.”
- “Síguenos si quieres entender antes de decidir.”
- “La herramienta gratuita está en el perfil.”

Avoid mechanical CTA repetition on every post.

## 20. Product/utility architecture

SIGMA is the audience-facing media brand.

Herramientas Rentables remains an existing owned property for tools and commercial utility during transition.

Working future presentation, only after explicit migration approval:

> **Herramientas Rentables by SIGMA**

This wording is not yet authorized for live-site replacement.

## 21. Trust standard

SIGMA must be more reliable than a generic social account.

Rules:

- material claims require sources according to QA tier;
- calculations must be reproducible;
- uncertainty must be stated;
- corrections must be logged;
- sponsored/commercial relationships must be clear;
- sources should be primary where practical;
- no invented statistics;
- no fake expert attribution.

## 22. Cultural-risk rule: “sigma” slang

Because `sigma` is widely used online as “sigma male” slang/meme culture, brand behavior must actively establish a different semantic association.

We do this through:

- evidence-led content;
- neutral/inclusive language;
- knowledge/decision positioning;
- no masculinity hierarchy language;
- no lone-wolf/grindset visuals;
- no ironic adoption of that meme during brand formation.

If audience comments repeatedly confuse the brand with the meme, track the rate and test a clarifying profile descriptor rather than changing positioning impulsively.

## 23. Brand QA checklist

A piece passes SIGMA Brand QA only if:

- [ ] it delivers practical value;
- [ ] the hook is supported by the content;
- [ ] claims meet the correct QA tier;
- [ ] tone is clear, intelligent and non-hype;
- [ ] it fits the editorial boundary;
- [ ] it is visually readable on mobile;
- [ ] color use follows accessibility rules;
- [ ] no accidental “sigma male” framing appears;
- [ ] CTA is appropriate and not manipulative;
- [ ] SIGMA is the dominant master brand;
- [ ] any Herramientas Rentables reference is treated as a utility/product property, not a competing master identity.

## 25. Definition of Minimum Viable Brand completion

The strategic brand system is complete enough for content creation and closed QA.

Public launch/rebrand eligibility still requires:

1. exact selected logo asset ingested and verified;
2. final social handle availability checked;
3. profile visuals rendered;
4. closed QA on first publication set;
5. no claim of legal trademark exclusivity unless separately cleared.

This preserves speed without pretending unresolved items are complete.
