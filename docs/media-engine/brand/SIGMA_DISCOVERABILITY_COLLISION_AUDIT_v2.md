## Executive result

The master brand remains **SIGMA** by user decision.

However, a naked `SIGMA` identifier is highly crowded across technology, media, industrial services, software/data and photography. This creates a discoverability/SEO ambiguity even before trademark law is considered.

Therefore the growth system should distinguish between:

- **master/display brand:** SIGMA
- **distinctive discovery identifier / handle family:** a more unique SIGMA compound
- **editorial signature:** `Lo que importa, explicado.`

This audit does not grant trademark clearance and does not prove handle/domain availability.

## Growth mitigation rules

### Display name

Prefer a contextual display identity such as:

`SIGMA | Lo que importa, explicado.`

when platform mechanics allow a descriptor without making the logo/brand name itself longer.

### Handle

Prefer a single coherent handle family across Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn rather than three unrelated variants.

Do not freeze the handle from search-engine absence.

### Search / SEO

Editorial pages and owned assets should avoid relying on the single keyword `SIGMA` for discovery. Pair it with topical meaning:

- SIGMA dinero
- SIGMA negocios
- SIGMA tecnología
- SIGMA decisiones
- SIGMA herramientas
- `Lo que importa, explicado.`

This is a discoverability tactic, not a renaming of the master brand.

### Social content

Use consistent visual identity, recurring franchises and the tagline so repeated exposure teaches the audience what this SIGMA means.

The goal is to make the compound identity distinctive through behavior and content, not to compete for generic `SIGMA` search intent alone.
