## Purpose

Reduce avoidable brand-collision risk before public rebranding. This document is an operational screening, not a legal opinion and not a substitute for a professional trademark clearance.

## Key findings

### 1. Plain `SIGMA` is highly congested

The name SIGMA is already used by multiple active companies internationally and in Chile, including technology, mining, software and other sectors. This makes a generic social identifier such as `@sigma` strategically weak even if a platform happened to expose it.

Operational decision: **do not build the public identity around a bare `@sigma` handle.** The display brand can remain SIGMA while handles use a distinctive modifier.

### 2. Chilean trademark collision risk is material

Public INAPI-derived records show historical and/or active SIGMA registrations in classes relevant to our future activity, including class 41 (education/training) and class 42 (technology/software). Public INAPI daily-state material also references prior SIGMA registrations in class 35 and 41 when analyzing confusing similarity.

Operational decision: **SIGMA_TRADEMARK_LEGAL_CLEARANCE remains PENDING_SPECIALIST_REVIEW.** No filing, paid redesign, mass public rename, domain portfolio purchase or other costly irreversible action should be justified by an assumption that SIGMA is legally clear.

### 3. `sigma.cl` is not a viable target

Historical NIC Chile dispute material records an existing long-term assignment of `sigma.cl` to another SIGMA rights holder.

Operational decision: reject `sigma.cl` as a target.
