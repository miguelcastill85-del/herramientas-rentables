# SIGMANOUVA BRAND KIT v1

Status: APPROVED_SPEC
Date: 2026-09-05
Authority: MEDIA_ENGINE_WORKLOG_v1.1_GROWTH_PATCH + DEC-0003

## 1. Brand role

SIGMANOUVA is the master audience-facing media and knowledge brand.

Purpose: make useful complexity understandable and memorable.

It must feel like a modern knowledge company: intelligent, useful, contemporary, premium, calm and evidence-led.

It must not feel like a motivational account, gaming clan, cybersecurity shield, crypto-hype brand, luxury-fashion label or generic AI content farm.

## 2. Positioning

For Spanish-speaking people who want to make better decisions in money, business, work and technology, SIGMANOUVA identifies what matters, verifies it and explains it clearly enough to use.

## 3. Brand promise

> **SIGMANOUVA convierte información compleja o poco explicada en ideas claras y útiles para tomar mejores decisiones.**

## 4. Tagline system

Primary tagline:

> **Lo que importa, explicado.**

Secondary signature:

> **Entender mejor. Decidir mejor.**

Do not stack both in small social assets.

## 5. Personality

- 70% Sage — evidence, clarity, explanation, precision.
- 20% Explorer — curiosity, discovery, overlooked mechanisms.
- 10% Challenger — question assumptions without theatrics.

SIGMANOUVA is clear, useful, precise, calm, curious, modern and confident without arrogance.

Avoid guru language, fake urgency, exaggerated certainty, pseudo-luxury posturing and clickbait conspiracies.

## 6. Voice

Default structure:

1. hook;
2. concrete claim;
3. example/number;
4. mechanism;
5. implication;
6. useful takeaway.

Platform tone:

- Instagram: immediate, visual, memorable.
- Facebook: explanatory, conversational, shareable.
- LinkedIn: professional, analytical, human.

Approved language examples:

- “Lo importante aquí es…”
- “La cuenta cambia cuando…”
- “Parece pequeño, pero…”
- “Esto ocurre porque…”
- “En la práctica…”
- “Antes de decidir, mira esto.”

Avoid unsupported phrases such as “nadie quiere que sepas esto”, “garantizado”, “hazte rico”, “dinero fácil” or “te están robando”.

## 7. Editorial territory

Core domains:

- pricing, margins and costs;
- freelancing and small business;
- sales and negotiation;
- consumer economics;
- marketing mechanisms;
- behavioral economics;
- AI and practical technology;
- digital tools;
- productivity with measurable utility;
- fees, rules and mechanisms that affect decisions;
- practical data interpretation.

Exclude as core strategy:

- generic motivation;
- celebrity gossip;
- partisan bait;
- random trivia;
- copied content;
- pseudo-science;
- get-rich-quick content;
- low-effort AI filler.

## 8. Editorial franchises

1. **Lo que nadie te explica**
2. **Errores caros**
3. **Cómo funciona realmente**
4. **El número que importa**
5. **Herramienta que vale conocer**
6. **Una idea para guardar**
7. **Caso / experimento**

All franchises are subordinate to SIGMANOUVA.

## 9. Canonical visual direction

The user-selected direction uses a sharp angular folded-metal **S** symbol with triangular planes, negative-space cuts and bronze/gold + dark graphite surfaces.

The mark should communicate:

- precision;
- intelligence;
- forward movement;
- synthesis;
- premium quality;
- modernity.

The wordmark uses **SIGMANOUVA** in a geometric sans-serif with wide tracking. The preferred first-letter `S` follows the user-selected squared/modern S style.

## 10. Logo family

The five generated branches are not five unrelated identities. They form one operational family.

### L01 — Hero / premium

Use:

- website hero;
- presentations;
- premium covers;
- launch graphics;
- brand decks.

Characteristics:

- emblem large;
- strongest metallic treatment;
- full wordmark;
- generous negative space.

### L02 — Instagram profile / square social

Use:

- Instagram profile tests;
- square profile-safe assets;
- story highlights / discovery covers where the full lockup is still readable.

Characteristics:

- emblem dominant;
- simplified lighting;
- full wordmark secondary.

At very small avatar sizes, prefer the emblem alone once a clean core export exists.

### L03 — Facebook horizontal

Use:

- Facebook header areas;
- page graphics;
- horizontal branded content.

Characteristics:

- emblem left;
- wordmark right;
- moderate metallic treatment;
- high legibility.

### L04 — LinkedIn horizontal corporate

Use:

- LinkedIn company/page branding;
- professional banners;
- executive documents.

Characteristics:

- restrained metallic treatment;
- strong spacing;
- corporate readability;
- no decorative excess.

### L05 — Universal simplified/core

Use:

- watermark;
- small content signatures;
- favicon/app-icon derivation;
- templates;
- internal tooling;
- future monochrome exports.

Characteristics:

- flatter gold/graphite treatment;
- minimal glow;
- crisp geometry;
- best scalability.

## 11. Social assignment

Instagram:

- profile: L02 initially; transition to symbol-only derivative after small-size QA.
- post covers: L05 mark as subtle signature.
- launch/premium campaign: L01 selectively.

Facebook:

- page/header graphics: L03.
- profile: symbol-only derivative or L02 depending crop/legibility.

LinkedIn:

- page/company header/brand lockup: L04.
- profile/company avatar: symbol-only derivative.

Owned web / products:

- L01 for hero presentation.
- L03/L04 for horizontal placements.
- L05 for functional/system contexts.

## 12. Color system

Keep the approved neutral architecture:

- Ink: `#111214`
- Ivory: `#F7F5F0`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Graphite: `#42454A`
- Gold accent: `#B88A2A`
- Deep Gold: `#7A571A`

The metallic hero render may contain natural bronze/champagne gradients beyond these flat colors, but content templates must remain restrained.

## 13. Typography

Logo typography is part of the final artwork and must not be approximated casually.

Supporting content fonts:

- Headlines: Manrope 600–800.
- Body: Inter 400–600.

No more than two supporting families in one asset.

## 14. Profile promise

Short recurring benefit:

Longer profile proposition:

## 15. CTA hierarchy

1. understand the idea;
2. save/share if genuinely useful;
3. follow for recurring value;
4. visit a free owned utility;
5. paid product only when contextually relevant.

Approved examples:

- “Guárdalo para cuando tengas que calcularlo.”
- “Síguenos si quieres entender antes de decidir.”
- “La herramienta gratuita está en el perfil.”

## 17. Logo QA rules

Before any logo variant is frozen as canonical binary:

- verify exact spelling `SIGMANOUVA`;
- check symbol geometry at small size;
- check circular avatar crop;
- check 1:1, 4:3 and banner placements;
- verify sufficient light/dark contrast;
- ensure no text clipping;
- ensure the angular S remains recognizable;
- record SHA-256 of final exported binaries;
- do not substitute a visually similar generated image without a new approval.

## 18. Naming-risk guardrail

Exact `SIGMANOUVA` web screening found no clear indexed collision on 2026-09-05.

The adjacent name `Sigma Nova / Sigma-Nova` has active uses, including construction in Chile and an AI lab in France. Therefore brand operation may continue, but legal clearance and direct handle/domain checks remain mandatory before costly or irreversible migration.
