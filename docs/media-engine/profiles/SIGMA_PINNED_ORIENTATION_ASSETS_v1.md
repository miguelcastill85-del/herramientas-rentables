# SIGMA PINNED ORIENTATION ASSETS v1

Status: APPROVED_COPY_SPEC
Date: 2026-09-05
Authority: SIGMA_BRAND_SYSTEM_v1 + PROFILE_CONVERSION_v2_SIGMA

## Purpose

A new visitor should understand SIGMA in seconds and have three different reasons to stay:

1. understand the recurring promise;
2. see proof that the content is useful;
3. discover deeper free utility without being pushed directly into a sale.

These are orientation assets, not permanent sacred posts. PIN-02 in particular should later be replaced by a proven organic winner.

---

## PIN-01 — WHY FOLLOW SIGMA

### Goal

Convert profile curiosity into a follow.

### Primary format

Carousel / short profile-introduction Reel.

### Cover

`No necesitas más información.`
`Necesitas entender qué importa.`

### Core copy

`SIGMA explica dinero, negocios, trabajo y tecnología práctica sin humo.`

`Desarmamos cálculos, decisiones y mecanismos que suelen estar mal explicados o escondidos a plena vista.`

`No prometemos hacerte rico.`
`No publicamos motivación vacía.`
`No convertimos rumores en hechos.`

`Cada pieza debe dejarte una idea que puedas usar, comprobar o guardar.`

### Closing

`SIGMA — Lo que importa, explicado.`

CTA: `Síguenos si prefieres entender antes de decidir.`

### QA

- avoid attacking other creators;
- do not claim exclusivity such as “somos los únicos”;
- no sales link on the artwork.

---

## PIN-02 — PROOF OF VALUE

### Initial authority

Use KV-000002 discount-volume math as the launch proof piece unless another Cohort 001 item wins before the profile is finalized.

### Cover option

`10% de descuento puede exigirte mucho más de 10% en ventas extra.`

### Reason for selection

- instantly understandable business problem;
- contains a surprising but transparent calculation;
- demonstrates SIGMA's preferred method: claim -> numbers -> caveat -> useful decision rule;
- strong save/share potential.

### Orientation caption ending

`Este es el tipo de cosa que hacemos en SIGMA: tomar algo cotidiano, abrirlo y mostrar el cálculo que cambia la decisión.`

`Lo que importa, explicado.`

### Replacement rule

After enough data exists, replace PIN-02 with the highest-quality proven winner that also represents the brand accurately. Do not pin a viral piece if it attracts the wrong audience or misrepresents the editorial territory.

---

## PIN-03 — FREE UTILITY

### Goal

Introduce the owned-asset layer without turning the profile into a sales catalog.

### Working positioning

`Entender está bien.`
`Poder calcularlo tú mismo es mejor.`

### Core copy

`SIGMA también conecta conocimiento con herramientas prácticas.`

`Durante la transición, parte de esas herramientas viven en Herramientas Rentables, nuestra propiedad de utilidad digital.`

`Úsalas para llevar una idea a tus propios números.`

### CTA

`Explora las herramientas gratuitas desde el enlace del perfil.`

### Commercial guardrail

- default destination must be a useful owned page, not a hard-sell checkout;
- paid products may exist downstream but should not dominate the first click;
- preserve existing UTM campaigns when routing to current Herramientas Rentables assets.

---

## Platform adaptation

### Instagram

Pin all three where supported. Optimize cover readability and visual differentiation while retaining the same SIGMA system.

### Facebook

Feature the promise post and utility post where page mechanics allow. Proof-of-value can be a top-performing native Reel/post.

### LinkedIn

Feature the brand explanation, one authority piece and a useful owned-resource post. Keep the tone professional and less promotional.
