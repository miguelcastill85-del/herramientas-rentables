# DEC-0002 — SIGMA Brand Architecture v1

Status: APPROVED_WITH_CLEARANCE_GUARDRAIL
Date: 2026-09-05
Authority: MEDIA_ENGINE_WORKLOG_v1.1_GROWTH_PATCH
Supersedes: DEC-0001 for all forward-looking brand identity decisions.

## Decision

Adopt **SIGMA** as the forward-looking master media brand for the Media Engine.

The existing **Herramientas Rentables** property remains a durable utility/product asset and is not deleted, renamed, repriced or republished automatically by this decision.

During transition, the architecture is:

- **SIGMA** — master media / audience / knowledge brand.
- **Herramientas Rentables** — existing tools, calculators, commercial pages and utility property; may be presented later as `Herramientas Rentables by SIGMA` only after explicit migration work.
- Existing public URLs, Payhip products, prices, UTMs, formulas and live-site behavior remain unchanged until a separately authorized migration.

## Why this supersedes DEC-0001

DEC-0001 selected Herramientas Rentables as the master brand to maximize launch speed. The user has now made an explicit strategic brand decision to use SIGMA as the master brand. This is a strategic Fast Follow exception authorized by the user before autonomous publishing begins, so the change is low-cost and reversible while still early.

## Positioning

SIGMA is a practical knowledge media brand that turns complex, dispersed or poorly explained information into clear, useful understanding for better decisions.

Primary territory:

- money and pricing;
- business and freelancing;
- work and negotiation;
- marketing and consumer economics;
- AI and practical technology;
- useful digital tools;
- behavioral economics and decision mechanisms;
- data, rules, fees and systems that materially change a decision.

## Brand promise

> SIGMA convierte información compleja o poco explicada en conocimiento práctico para tomar mejores decisiones.

## Primary tagline

> **Lo que importa, explicado.**

## Secondary signature

> **Entender mejor. Decidir mejor.**

The primary tagline is the default brand-facing line. The secondary signature is available for campaigns, profile copy and editorial sign-off; it must not compete visually with the primary tagline in the same small composition.

## Existing asset transition rule

Herramientas Rentables keeps its current role as an owned utility/commercial property while SIGMA develops the audience-facing media identity.

No automatic changes are authorized to:

- public site name or slug;
- current site URL/domain;
- calculator formulas;
- Payhip products or prices;
- UTM campaigns;
- sitemap or indexed pages;
- product names;
- existing published content.

Any public migration must be separately specified, tested and reversible.

## Name-clearance guardrail

SIGMA is operationally approved as the working/master brand, but **legal exclusivity is NOT declared**.

The term is already used by multiple active technology/media/business organizations and has trademark activity in Chile. Therefore:

- `BRAND_NAME_OPERATING_APPROVAL = PASS`
- `TRADEMARK_LEGAL_CLEARANCE = PENDING_SPECIALIST_REVIEW`
- `HANDLE_AND_DOMAIN_CLEARANCE = PENDING`

This guardrail prevents a false statement that the name is legally free while allowing non-public brand-system and content preparation to continue.

Before a costly public rebrand, paid trademark filing, packaging, large ad spend or irreversible migration, perform jurisdiction-appropriate trademark clearance and handle/domain checks.

## Cultural association guardrail

The word `sigma` also has a widespread internet-slang association with the “sigma male” meme/archetype. The brand must deliberately distance itself from manosphere, pseudo-scientific hierarchy, macho-grindset and meme-bait positioning.

SIGMA means, in this brand system: synthesis, clarity, evidence, useful knowledge and better decisions.

Do not use:

- “sigma male” language;
- alpha/beta hierarchy framing;
- lone-wolf masculinity imagery;
- grindset clichés;
- phonk/macho meme aesthetics as master-brand language.
