# DEC-0003 — SIGMANOUVA Brand Architecture v1

Status: APPROVED_WITH_COLLISION_GUARDRAIL
Date: 2026-09-05
Authority: MEDIA_ENGINE_WORKLOG_v1.1_GROWTH_PATCH
Supersedes: DEC-0002 for all forward-looking brand identity decisions.

## Decision

Adopt **SIGMANOUVA** as the forward-looking master media brand for the Media Engine.

The existing **Herramientas Rentables** property remains a durable utility/product asset and is not deleted, renamed, repriced or republished automatically by this decision.

Forward architecture:

- **SIGMANOUVA** — master media / audience / knowledge brand.
- **Herramientas Rentables** — existing calculators, tools, commercial pages and utility property.
- Existing public URLs, Payhip products, prices, UTMs, formulas, sitemap and live-site behavior remain unchanged until a separately authorized migration.

## Why this supersedes SIGMA

The user explicitly replaced SIGMA with SIGMANOUVA before controlled publication began. This is still pre-launch identity work, so migration cost is low and no scientific/content evidence is being rewritten.

SIGMANOUVA retains continuity with the SIGMA concept while becoming a more distinctive audience-facing name.

## Positioning

SIGMANOUVA is a practical knowledge media brand that turns complex, dispersed, overlooked or poorly explained information into clear, useful understanding for better decisions.

Primary territory:

- money, margins, pricing and costs;
- business and freelancing;
- negotiation and sales;
- consumer economics;
- AI and practical technology;
- useful digital tools;
- behavioral economics and decision mechanisms;
- data, rules, fees and systems that materially change a decision.

## Brand promise

> **SIGMANOUVA convierte información compleja o poco explicada en ideas claras y útiles para tomar mejores decisiones.**

## Primary tagline

> **Lo que importa, explicado.**

## Secondary signature

> **Entender mejor. Decidir mejor.**

## Logo direction

The selected visual direction is the angular folded-metal **S** symbol preferred by the user, paired with the SIGMANOUVA wordmark.

The identity family must contain distinct operational variants rather than unrelated logos:

1. Hero / premium lockup.
2. Social-square / profile lockup.
3. Horizontal Facebook lockup.
4. Horizontal corporate LinkedIn lockup.
5. Universal simplified/core lockup.

The symbol remains recognizable across all five.

## Existing asset transition rule

No automatic public changes are authorized to:

- site name, slug or public URL;
- calculator formulas;
- Payhip products or prices;
- existing UTM campaigns;
- sitemap or indexed pages;
- published Herramientas Rentables content.

Any public migration must be separately specified, tested and reversible.
