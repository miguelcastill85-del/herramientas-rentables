## Description

Freelance Pricing Calculator & Profit Tracker for Microsoft Excel.

Build a sustainable hourly rate, price projects with a protected floor and target margin, see the maximum safe discount before you negotiate, then compare quoted work with the actual result after delivery.

### What you can do

- Calculate a sustainable hourly rate from annual income goal, overhead, reserve assumptions, available weeks and realistic billable utilization.
- Build project quotes with complexity, rush, external costs, contingency and target margin.
- See protected cost floor, recommended project price, proposed final price and maximum safe discount.
- Record estimated vs actual hours and revenue collected.
- Review effective hourly rate and economic surplus versus the sustainable floor.
- Build pricing memory by service type from completed projects.
- Monitor pricing health in a compact dashboard.

### You receive

- 1 Microsoft Excel `.xlsx` workbook.
- 1 `START_HERE.txt` quick-start file.
- 9 workbook modules: Start Here, Setup, Rate Builder, Quote Builder, Projects, Pricing Memory, Dashboard, Example and Methodology.

### Compatibility

Designed and validated as a Microsoft Excel `.xlsx` workbook. No macros, paid add-ins or subscription are required. Google Sheets compatibility is not claimed in this release until separately validated.

### Important

This is a digital download; no physical item will be shipped.

This workbook is a business-planning and pricing tool. It does not provide tax, legal, accounting or financial advice. Tax and reserve percentages are assumptions entered by the user and results depend on those inputs.
